Privacy policy

Josh Brett Studio and the "Studio bridge" Google Apps Script. Last updated 10 November 2026.

Who runs this

Josh Brett Studio is an internal tool run by Liftkeeper Ltd, a UK limited company, for Josh Brett and his team. It is not a public service and only people with the team's access code can use it.

What it uses

To show the team's dashboard, the site reads and updates data that already belongs to the business:

  • Google Sheets owned by Josh Brett (content plan, sponsorship tracker and production tasks), through the Studio bridge Apps Script.
  • Business emails in Josh Brett's Zoho Mail account that relate to sponsorships, so they can be listed against the right brand.
  • Public information about videos on Josh Brett's YouTube channel.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell, share or pass any of this data to anyone outside the team.
  • It does not use the data for advertising.
  • It does not collect personal data from visitors. The only thing stored in your browser is the access code, if you choose to stay signed in on that device.

Google user data

The Studio bridge script only accesses the Google account of its owner, Josh Brett, and only the spreadsheets the dashboard needs. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party apps and services), which stops the script reading any data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Josh Brett through brettjosh.com.
